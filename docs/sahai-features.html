Sahai — Feature Reference

Algoflow AI's Hospital Information Management System, integrated with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. Reference document for the Sahai voice assistant's knowledge base.

What Sahai is[slide:intro]

Sahai is an AI-powered Hospital Information Management System (HIMS) built by Algoflow AI for Indian hospitals. Sahai is integrated with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and supports ABHA health account creation, patient consent, and health record exchange as standard functionality rather than as an add-on.

Sahai is delivered as a multi-tenant cloud platform. Each hospital registers as its own tenant with its own staff accounts, branding and data. Sahai covers the full patient pathway from registration and appointment booking, through outpatient and inpatient care, diagnostics, pharmacy and theatre, to billing and discharge. Engagements include implementation, data migration, staff training and ongoing support.

Patient records[slide:patients]

Sahai's patient module is the central record for every person the hospital treats. Each patient is given a unique hospital identifier (UHID) and a patient profile that carries demographics, contact details, blood group, and linked ABHA identifiers where the patient has one.

What the patient module provides

  • A searchable patient directory, with lookup by name, mobile number or ABHA identifier.
  • A printable patient ID card carrying a QR code, so a returning patient can be identified by scanning rather than by re-entering details.
  • Kiosk-style check-in: staff can scan a patient's QR code or an existing ABHA card, search by mobile number, or register a new patient when no record is found.
  • A patient timeline consolidating appointments, OPD and IPD episodes, lab tests, invoices, video consultations and discharges into one chronological view.
  • Document storage for lab reports, prescriptions, invoices, discharge summaries and theatre reports.
  • Recorded patient consent for treatment, billing and ABDM record sharing, with the ability to withdraw consent later.

ABHA registration[slide:abha]

Sahai can create an ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) for a patient during registration, without leaving the system. Sahai's ABHA registration runs as a guided four-step wizard.

The four steps of ABHA registration in Sahai

  • Mobile Check — find an existing ABHA and any existing patient record.
  • Aadhaar Verification — verify the patient's identity with an Aadhaar OTP.
  • ABHA Address — confirm the patient's profile and choose an ABHA address.
  • ABHA Card — issue the health card and its QR code.

Sahai can also look up an existing ABHA by ABHA number or by mobile number, so a patient who already has an account does not have to create a second one.

ABDM integration[slide:abdm]

Sahai's ABDM module connects the hospital to the national digital health ecosystem. It handles the consent lifecycle and the exchange of health records with other ABDM-registered providers.

What the ABDM module provides

  • Consent management: raise consent requests, track their status, and honour withdrawals.
  • Care-context linking, so a patient's episodes at this hospital are discoverable.
  • Fetching patient health data from other providers once consent is granted, and viewing the fetched health records against the patient's profile.
  • Pushing documents generated at this hospital back into the ABDM ecosystem.
  • Notifications when a consent request arrives or its status changes.

Outpatient and emergency[slide:opd]

Sahai manages the outpatient department as a live queue. The OPD screen shows who is waiting, who is currently in consultation and who has been seen, so front-desk staff and clinicians work from the same view.

What OPD and Emergency provide

  • A first-come-first-served token queue for the day, with priority handling.
  • Queue management: move a patient between waiting, in-consultation and completed states.
  • A separate emergency triage queue tracking patients who are waiting, under treatment or discharged, with critical cases flagged.
  • Consultation notes and prescriptions recorded against the visit and stored on the patient's record.

Inpatient, beds and theatre[slide:ipd]

Sahai's inpatient module covers a patient's stay from admission to discharge, and includes a live bed board so staff can see ward occupancy at a glance.

What the inpatient module provides

  • Admissions, with the patient assigned to a specific bed.
  • A bed board showing occupancy across wards, and actions to assign or vacate a bed as patients move.
  • Configurable ward and bed types, each with its own daily rate set by the hospital.
  • Transfers between wards, and transfers to the operation theatre.
  • Nursing records and patient vitals recorded through the stay.
  • Discharge handling, including the discharge summary and, where required, a death certificate.
  • Operation theatre scheduling with a surgery schedule view.

Pharmacy and laboratory[slide:pharmacy]

Sahai includes the hospital's own pharmacy and diagnostic laboratory rather than treating them as external systems, so medicines and test results stay attached to the patient record.

Pharmacy

  • Medicine inventory, with stock tracked per item.
  • Expiry monitoring, highlighting medicines approaching their expiry date.
  • Dispensing against a patient's prescription.

Laboratory

  • A configurable catalogue of tests the hospital offers, each with the hospital's own price.
  • A staged order pipeline: payment pending, sample collection, then completion and report upload.
  • Report upload against the order, filed to the patient's documents.

Care pathways and the patient journey[slide:journey]

Sahai's care pathways feature gives each patient a structured clinical journey rather than a loose collection of visits. A department defines the stages a patient should pass through, and Sahai tracks each patient against that pathway.

The patient journey view

For an individual patient, Sahai assembles a single clinical picture drawing together:

  • A patient summary and their chief complaints.
  • Vitals and clinical history.
  • Investigations, including lab orders and their reports.
  • Diagnoses and treatment plans.
  • Current and historic medications, and historic prescriptions.
  • Procedures and surgeries.
  • Responses to department questionnaires.
  • An AI-generated clinical summary of the record.

Configuring care pathways

  • Journey templates — per department, a named template composed of ordered stages, each stage marked required or repeatable, and each template active or inactive.
  • Journey stage library — the reusable stages that templates are built from.
  • Department questionnaires — per department, a titled questionnaire with its own fields, completed as part of the journey.

Appointments and video consultations[slide:video]

Sahai handles booking for both in-person and remote consultations, and hosts the video call itself rather than handing off to a third-party meeting tool.

What appointments and video consultations provide

  • Booking against a doctor's configured availability, with appointment types covering OPD, emergency, video, follow-up and walk-in.
  • Calendar and list views of the schedule, plus editing and cancellation.
  • Doctor schedules defining availability windows and bookable slots.
  • Video consultations run inside Sahai, with the patient joining from a link sent to them — no account or app install required on the patient's side.
  • Chat and file sharing alongside the video call.

Billing and payments[slide:billing]

Sahai consolidates charges from every part of the hospital — consultations, inpatient stays, pharmacy and laboratory — into the patient's bill.

What billing provides

  • Invoices drawing on charges from across all modules, with an all-invoices view.
  • Online payment through Razorpay, alongside cash, card, UPI and payment links, with receipts and refunds.
  • Automatic calculation of an inpatient bill from the stay and the bed rates applied.
  • A patient wallet balance and a transaction ledger.
  • Tracking of pending dues against collected revenue.

Reports and analytics[slide:reports]

Sahai's reporting gives hospital management an operational and financial picture drawn from live data rather than manual returns.

What reporting provides

  • A live patient census across outpatient, emergency, inpatient, intensive care and theatre.
  • Revenue analytics over a selectable period, with monthly revenue trends.
  • Totals for revenue collected, dues outstanding, patients and appointments.
  • Doctor utilisation and the outpatient-to-inpatient mix.
  • Export to PDF for circulation.

Staff, roles and access control[slide:platform]

Sahai is role-based: each staff member sees the parts of the system their role requires and lands on the screen relevant to their job.

The roles Sahai supports

  • SuperAdmin — platform level, across hospitals.
  • Admin — full access within their hospital.
  • Doctor — clinical workspace, their own schedule and patients.
  • Nurse — ward, vitals and nursing records.
  • Receptionist — registration, appointments and front desk.
  • Pharmacist — pharmacy inventory and dispensing.
  • Pathologist — laboratory orders and reports.

Staff sign in with a mobile OTP or with an email and password. Sessions use short-lived tokens that are rotated automatically. Staff belonging to more than one hospital choose which one they are working in at sign-in.

Hospital setup and branding[slide:platform]

Sahai is multi-tenant. A hospital registers itself, is approved at the platform level, and then configures its own environment.

What a hospital can configure

  • Hospital profile: name, ABDM health facility identifier, address, departments and bed count.
  • White-label branding: logo, brand name, tagline, primary colour, letterhead and footer, which are applied across the interface and onto generated documents.
  • Report and invoice formats.
  • Ward and bed types with their daily rates.
  • The laboratory test catalogue and its pricing.
  • Notification preferences and security settings.

Plans and subscription[slide:platform]

Sahai is sold as a subscription per hospital. Pricing is published on the platform and a hospital selects its plan when registering.

How subscription works

  • A hospital chooses a plan during registration, before any staff account exists.
  • Payment is taken online through Razorpay, by order or by payment link.
  • A subscription runs for a term and carries a status — awaiting payment, active, payment failed, refunded, or referred to sales.
  • Larger or non-standard deployments are handled by contacting sales.
  • Plan pricing is maintained at the platform level and is publicly readable.

AI in Sahai[slide:intro]

Sahai embeds AI assistance in clinical and administrative work rather than offering it as a separate product.

  • An AI assistant that answers questions about a patient in the context of their record.
  • AI-generated patient summaries and clinical summaries of a health record.
  • AI assistance for discharge documentation and prescriptions.
  • This voice assistant, which answers questions about Sahai itself.
